About

Genomic evidence for transmission, resistance and public-health response.

Rhys White, PhD, MASM, MRSNZ

I am a Scientist in Genomics & Bioinformatics, Health Security at the New Zealand Institute for Public Health and Forensic Science (PHF Science).

My work sits between sequencing and action: I analyse bacterial genomes to resolve whether cases are linked, how antimicrobial resistance is moving, and what the evidence supports.

Projects connect hospital-generated nanopore sequencing with reference-laboratory context, comparative genomics, and clinical or infection-prevention interpretation.

I also develop research software when an analytical step needs explicit validation, uncertainty or reproducible provenance.

About this site. This is my personal research website. Research Notes and commentary are my own summaries of published collaborative work. They are not official communications from PHF Science or any other organisation with which I am affiliated.
